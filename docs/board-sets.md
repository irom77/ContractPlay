# Bundled board sets

## Ruffing in the short hand — Level 1

ContractPlay includes an eight-board PBN practice set adapted from the first level of [Bridge Training's Ruffing in the short hand collection](https://www.bridge-training.com/en/themes/short-hand-ruff/), by Jean-Pierre Desmoulins. The file keeps the deals, auctions, contracts, and recorded opening leads. It omits the site's explanatory review text.

The World Bridge Federation Academy says Bridge Training content is published under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Bridge Training's [Terms of Use, Articles 8–10](https://bridge-training.com/en/learn/terms/) define deals, card distributions, auctions, play, and related teaching material as Bridge Content, require authors to hold the necessary rights, and apply the CC0 dedication to submitted Bridge Content. The downloadable PBN also includes the source link on every board and a `License` tag.

The source copy is `apps/client/src/fixtures/bridge-training-ruffing-short-hand-level-1.pbn`.
